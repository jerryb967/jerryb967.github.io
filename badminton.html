About the Game

Badminton is a fast racket sport powered by reflexes, placement, and timing.

You can play singles (1v1) or doubles (2v2). Unlike many racket sports, players hit a shuttlecock that can exceed 300 km/h in elite play.

Quick Rules

Core badminton rules every club member should know.

  • Matches are best of 3 games to 21 points.
  • You must win by 2 points (cap at 30 in a game).
  • A point is scored on every rally (rally-point system).
  • At 11 points, players get a short mid-game interval.
  • Sides are changed at the end of each game.
Serving & Scoring

Serve diagonally and keep the shuttle below waist level when striking.

In doubles, teammates alternate service courts after winning points on serve. In singles, even score serves from the right service court, odd from the left.

Equipment & Training

Start with a light racket, court shoes, and consistent footwork drills.

Club sessions include warmups, shadow footwork, drop-shot practice, and friendly matches. Beginners can progress quickly with regular movement and control training.
